Delete your account

You do not need to be signed in on this page.

Email admin@yawar.app from the email on your Yawar account. Use the subject “Delete my Yawar account”. If you signed in with a phone number, put that number in the email.

What we delete

  • Your profile, name, phone, and email.
  • Memberships, class reservations, and course enrollments.
  • Shop orders tied to the account.
  • Chat messages, progress photos, weight logs, and notification tokens.

What we may keep

We may keep a short record if a law, a gym dispute, or a payment dispute requires it. We do not keep it for advertising.

How long it takes

We delete the account within 7 days and reply to the same email when it is done. Deletion is permanent.
