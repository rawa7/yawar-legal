Support

Rawa Software Studio, Erbil

Email admin@yawar.app. Write in English, Arabic, or Kurdish. Include the email or phone number on your Yawar account so we can find it.

We can help with

  • Signing in, or a membership that does not show on your card.
  • A class reservation or a course that should still be open.
  • A shop order, including cash on delivery.
  • A gym or shop listing that needs a correction.
  • Deleting your account. Use the delete account page.

Payments

Yawar does not charge a card inside the app. Memberships are paid at the gym. Shop orders are cash on delivery. If a listed price looks wrong, the gym or the shop confirms it at the desk.
