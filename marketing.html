Gyms, courses, and shops in Erbil.

Yawar is the fitness app for Erbil. Find a gym on the map, follow a course, and order from a local shop.

In the app

  • Real gyms, with addresses, hours, and membership plans in English, Arabic, and Kurdish.
  • Courses built from an exercise library, with an access period you can renew.
  • Protein, prepared meals, and training equipment from local shops.
  • A membership card you show at the gym.
  • Private progress photos and weight, unless you choose to share a transformation.

How you pay

This version does not charge a card in the app. You pay the gym at the desk, or you pay the shop when the order arrives.

From

Rawa Software Studio, Erbil. Questions go to admin@yawar.app. The support page and the privacy policy are public.
